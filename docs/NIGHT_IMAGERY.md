The map uses exactly **18,000 analytic lights with varied sizes**. Most lights now cover the land between cities; the city layer’s centers and relative footprints come from the earliest successful inline NightMap SVG, recovered from the original source change at 2026-11-13T18:52:54.353Z and its CSS. `public/trip/korea-svg-reference.json` is the preserved reference; `public/trip/korea-lights.json` is the generated geographic point field.

`node scripts/prepare-korea-lights.mjs` deterministically regenerates the field. A three-anchor affine registration maps the stylized SVG to Seoul, Busan and Jeju. The fixed budget is split into 12,240 subdued random land-coverage points and 5,755 city-weighted points plus five exact anchors. The coverage layer gives the previously empty interior a visible floor; the city mixture retains the original centers and radius-cubed weights. A soft approximate eastern ridge reduces density gradually instead of cutting a hard polygon. Land rejection keeps all points on Korea without snapping to coastlines. There is no placement grid. This is decorative geography, not measured population, terrain or street-light data.

The renderer draws all points in one geographic scene with the coastlines. Each small warm core has an analytic soft halo and bounded screen size during close zoom. Source-over blending keeps overlaps warm rather than accumulating to white. Ten faint geographic halos preserve the reference footprints. Selected endpoints and journey connections remain separate and coral red.

The intro uses the existing NASA Visible Earth day/night/cloud globe maps. Land and coastline geometry use Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0 coordinates, including China and Japan. `node scripts/prepare-planet-lods.mjs` prepares a shared red/green land mask for Korea and its unlit neighbors over 116–139°E / 27–44°N. Neighbors appear wherever they enter the actual camera frame; close-up zoom is retained.

Old VIIRS/ISS imagery and the vectorized globe SVG are offline reference artifacts only. The Korea/detail surface does not switch to a stitched photo atlas.

Provenance: original project SVG/CSS in the reference JSON; [Natural Earth public-domain map data](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/); [NASA Visible Earth](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/).
