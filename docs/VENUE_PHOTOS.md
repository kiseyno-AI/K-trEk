# Venue photo subject audit

Completed 2026-11-15 against the final 70-card manifest. **21 area-context substitutions and one Korea House wedding scene were replaced; all 70 cards now show their actual named subject.** The audit retained 48 already valid venue/area views, including the ten previously verified exact-clinic photographs. No generic stock or generated venue photo was used.

## What was verified

Each selected image was traced to its real source page and visually inspected. The existing Commons descriptions and categories were re-read for all 37 retained files. The 22 replacements were checked against operator/venue listing identity and branch information, then reviewed in a contact sheet. The card captions describe the visible subject in English. Named-area cards are distinguished from specific venues: an actual photograph of Myeongdong shopping street is valid for that title, while a Hongdae street is not valid for a restaurant, clinic or studio.

Important exact-branch checks include Hanilkwan Apgujeong main branch; Byeokje Galbi Bangi; Born & Bred Seoul New Hall; Hanboknam Gyeongbokgung; ADERERROR Seongsu Space (store 111); Olive Young N Seongsu; Musinsa Store Seongsu at Daelim Warehouse; HAUS NOWHERE DOSAN (not the distinct Seongsu HAUS NOWHERE SEOUL); BRANME Seongsu; Running Man Insadong; and the official 1MILLION studio.

## Subject limits kept explicit

- National Gugak Center is the real performance venue exterior. Its photo is not represented as a current Saturday-stage photograph.
- Seoul Sky uses the actual Lotte World Tower containing the observatory. Its caption explicitly describes the tower exterior rather than a view from the observation deck.
- The Paju DMZ card uses the actual Dora Observatory tour stop. Its archived exterior is not represented as a Third Tunnel interior.
- Samcheonggak shows the named dining venue buildings. Korea House now shows the actual Royal Cuisine Dining meal from its autumn 2026 announcement; its former wedding-courtyard image was rejected.
- Archived and seasonal images identify the real subject but do not establish current interiors, weather, menus, flowers or future cast availability.

## Source and delivery notes

All 22 replacements are controlled local WebP files. Two MICHELIN restaurant-listing images are credited as references via the MICHELIN Guide, not as photographs owned by the restaurant or this project. HiKR’s image is the official KTO article’s actual HiKR facade Open Graph photograph; an unrelated promotional banner was rejected. Hanboknam is cropped from the operator’s explicitly named branch image.

Balwoo Gongyang’s actual autumn 2026 dish image was read from the restaurant’s official menu page over HTTP because its HTTPS certificate did not validate. The local displayed image does not depend on that connection. The manifest records this source-access limitation and does not claim verified reuse permission. Its operator’s secure public information page also identifies Balwoo Gongyang, but the photo provenance remains the actual restaurant menu page.

All prior clinic-source rights distinctions are preserved: public official publication is not treated as an open reuse license. The eight existing remote clinic references retain their previously recorded cross-site HTTP 200 image-delivery checks; external hosts may change their files later.
