# Motion, data and public integration verification

Latest local v0.2.0 community/grid verification is in `docs/COMMUNITY_LOCAL_0_2_0.md`. It records compact card resource ownership, repeated detail navigation, bounded photo/DOM counts, measured renderer submission and the unavailable physical-device/whole-page profiling checks. This local change adds no GPU objects, polling or frame loop; production assets were not rebuilt.

## Ownership and budgets

One persistent WebGL2 canvas owns globe, Korea and detail states. Camera flights are finite RAF transitions from the currently displayed camera, including interruption/reversal. Intro waits 2.4 seconds after assets become ready and flies for 5.6 seconds; browsing opens after approximately 8.5 seconds. Detail travel is 2.7 seconds. Reduced motion bypasses the wait and movement.

One ambient timeout is capped at 12 draws/second and pauses during camera travel, hidden/offscreen scenes and reduced motion. Phase resumes after pausing. One IntersectionObserver watches the actual canvas. Cleanup aborts pending loads, closes decoded bitmaps, cancels RAF/timers, disconnects the observer, removes listeners and deletes owned GPU objects.

The GPU owner holds four textures, six buffers, four programs and eight shaders. Exactly 18,000 analytic warm point sprites share one buffer; ten faint halo quads use 60 vertices. Selected endpoint rings remain a separate pass. Coastlines and all points share one projection. Texture storage estimate is 41,943,041 bytes including mipmaps, not total GPU/process memory. Warm source-over blending avoids white additive overlap; sprite size caps in screen space during close zoom.

The card/panel owns one native Web Animations handle: 950ms opening and 750ms closing, using transforms/opacity. It cancels on replacement/unmount, pauses with document visibility and finishes when reduced motion is enabled. The carousel mounts only the focused card and its two neighbors on each side, at most five, instead of mounting all 70 images. Keyboard movement transfers focus to the new card. Short viewports use a compact card and single horizontally scrollable category row; the document never gains vertical scrolling.
