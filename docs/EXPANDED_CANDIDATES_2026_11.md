## Scope and import contract

Thirty new, distinct candidates: **14 food, 3 nightlife, 3 shopping and 10 experiences**. Regions include Seoul, Incheon, Gyeonggi, Daejeon, Chungbuk, Jeonbuk, Gangwon and Gyeongbuk. This dataset does not replace or update existing cards.

`node --experimental-strip-types scripts/import-expanded-candidates.mjs --check` validates the dataset and local WebP files. `--sql /tmp/ktravel-expanded-candidates-2026-11.sql` writes **INSERT OR IGNORE** statements only. The script never connects to or writes a database. Apply the generated SQL through the normal deployment owner after reviewing it. A later rerun preserves existing administrator edits, including comments and ratings.

Every pin was checked against an exact named Kakao Local public POI on 2026-11-16. Coordinates are WGS84/EPSG:4326. These are mapped POIs, not surveyed entrances, legal drop-off points or verified coach access. The full POI names, IDs, addresses and coordinates are in the photo manifest's `coordinates` list, including the card without a display image.

Actual driving times, Google ratings/review counts/Place IDs, Shawn/Sun comments and ratings remain null. Booking status stays unknown. Published class durations are separate from planning suggestions. English websites do not imply English staff or an English program. Group capacity, group discounts, ten seats together and visit-date confirmation remain distinct facts.
